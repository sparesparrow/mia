When They Watch You, You Watch Them Back

MIA is your co-driver: she sees who's behind you, reads what's going on under the bonnet, and does everything you'd otherwise take your hands off the wheel for. She runs on a Raspberry Pi under your seat, not in someone else's cloud, and sells your car's data to nobody.

  • Built on an Audi A4 Cabriolet 8H
  • Raspberry Pi 4B + ESP32 + Android
  • Read-only access to the vehicle

What MIA does

Arsenal for Digital Guerrilla

This is what MIA does while you keep your hands on the wheel.

Anti-Stalker Protocol

When the night fog lifts and your rear wiper catches the same number plate for the sixth time, MIA warns you: Heads up! Someone's riding your ass!

The Plate Black Book

Every number plate of the cars driving behind you goes into a database. If they keep showing up, you'll know.

Unmarked Cop Detector

Just because you're paranoid doesn't mean they're not after you: every plate you pass is checked against edalnice.cz. Vehicles exempt from the motorway toll are driven by the criminal police!

Hands on the Wheel

When you drive, your hands are on the wheel and your eyes on the road. Not even an automatic gearbox lets you fiddle with your phone's settings while driving; MIA does it for you, just talk to her. And when they pull you over, MIA will play your pregnant wife on the phone, the one you're speeding to get to the hospital!

The Car Talks, MIA Translates

RPM, coolant temperature, battery voltage, fault codes. MIA reads them from OBD and CAN, and when she doesn't like something she tells you out loud, instead of you hearing it from the tow truck driver. Hook up Torque or Car Scanner if you like: she passes for a plain ELM327 OBD adapter.

The Camera That Never Sleeps

Your phone records the road on a loop, deletes old footage by itself and puts the important clips aside. When someone rear-ends you and then claims you reversed into them, you play it back to them.

Steering-Wheel Buttons

MIA maps your steering-wheel buttons to any action you like: read out phone notifications when a message pings, start Waze navigation on a route with no cops, or send "RDY" to all your Signal contacts.

MIA in the Factory Speakers

Your 2004 radio has no Bluetooth? No problem. MIA plugs into the CD changer connector, the radio thinks it's playing “CD 1”, and music, navigation and MIA all come out of the factory speakers. No cut wires, no new head unit. And the steering-wheel buttons send her commands.

What already runs

No slide deck. This is written, tested in CI and running in simulation. We're bolting it into the car right now.

What's under the bonnet

RPM, coolant temperature, battery voltage and ignition, from OBD and passive CAN listening.

Read-only Audi

VAG diagnostics that only read. MIA keeps her hands off the control units.

Plate reader

A rear-facing camera reads number plates and checks them against eDálnice straight away.

Talk, don't fiddle

The Pi handles voice commands and the phone speaks the answers.

Phone as a dashboard

BLE, live telemetry, dashcam and your own “if this, do that” rules.

Brain under the seat

A Raspberry Pi with an API, WebSocket and services that start by themselves. No hardware attached? It runs in simulation.
