HANDS ON THE WHEEL

When you drive, your hands are on the wheel and your eyes on the road. Not even an automatic gearbox lets you fiddle with your phone's settings while driving; MIA does it for you, just talk to her. And when they pull you over, MIA will play your pregnant wife on the phone, the one you're speeding to get to the hospital!

"Say “Hey Mia” and leave the phone in its mount."

THE CAR TALKS, MIA TRANSLATES

RPM, coolant temperature, battery voltage, fault codes. MIA reads them from OBD and CAN, and when she doesn't like something she tells you out loud, instead of you hearing it from the tow truck driver. Hook up Torque or Car Scanner if you like: she passes for a plain ELM327 OBD adapter.

"She only listens. The ESP32 on the CAN bus doesn't even have a transmit path compiled in."
📹

THE CAMERA THAT NEVER SLEEPS

Your phone records the road on a loop, deletes old footage by itself and puts the important clips aside. When someone rear-ends you and then claims you reversed into them, you play it back to them.

"Memory has holes. Video doesn't."

JOIN THE PILOT WHAT ALREADY WORKS

DIGITAL GUERRILLA MANIFESTO

Your car knows more about you than your mum does. How much you drive, where you park, how you brake. A new car sends that to the manufacturer and the insurer. MIA reads it for you alone.

No cloud, no subscription. A Raspberry Pi under the seat, a couple of ESP32s behind the dash and a phone in the mount. The hardware is yours, the code is open, and nobody can switch it off remotely.

She only listens to the car. She sends nothing on the bus and writes nothing to the control units. MIA can't kill your engine or unlock your doors, even if she wanted to.

Join us. Or keep driving a car that snitches on you instead of to you.
