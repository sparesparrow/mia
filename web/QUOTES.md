# Quotes on the homepage (`#neposlusnost`)

Every quote in the "Neposlušnost: svoboda zespoda" section of `web/index.html` and `web/en/index.html`,
with the source it was checked against (27 November 2026). Change a quote only after reading it in a
primary source; libertarian quote collections are full of misattributed and "improved" lines.
Czech wording is our own translation unless noted.

1. **Étienne de La Boétie**, *Discours de la servitude volontaire* (written c. 1548, published 1576/77)
   - Original: "Soyez résolus de ne servir plus, et vous voilà libres."
   - Checked in: hurlus.fr edition of the 1576 text, p. 4 —
     <https://hurlus.fr/laboetie1562_servitude/laboetie1562_servitude.pdf>
   - CS: „Rozhodněte se, že už nebudete sloužit, a jste svobodní.“ EN (ours): "Be resolved to serve no more,
     and you are free." Harry Kurz's 1942 translation ("Resolve to serve no more…") is in copyright.

2. **Henry David Thoreau**, *Resistance to Civil Government* / *Civil Disobedience* (1849), paragraph 19
   - Original: "Let your life be a counter friction to stop the machine."
   - Checked in: Project Gutenberg #71 — <https://www.gutenberg.org/files/71/71-h/71-h.htm>
   - Some reprints hyphenate "counter-friction"; Gutenberg does not.

3. **Frédéric Bastiat**, *L'État* (1848), *Œuvres complètes*, 3rd ed., vol. 4
   - Original: "L'État, c'est la grande fiction à travers laquelle Tout Le Monde s'efforce de vivre aux dépens
     de Tout Le Monde."
   - EN (Liberty Fund): "The state is that great fiction by which everyone tries to live at the expense of
     everyone else." — <https://oll.libertyfund.org/quotes/145>
   - CS: „Stát je ta velká fikce, skrze kterou se každý snaží žít na úkor všech.“

4. **Ludwig von Mises**, *Liberty and Property* (1958), part V
   - Original: "Government is essentially the negation of liberty."
   - Checked in: *The Mises Reader Unabridged*, ch. 19 —
     <https://mises.org/online-book/mises-reader-unabridged/chapter-19-importance-liberty>
   - Context: in the same paragraph Mises calls government necessary and beneficial. He defended a minimal
     state, not anarchy; do not present him as an anarchist.

5. **Murray N. Rothbard**, *For a New Liberty* (1973), ch. 2 "Property and Exchange", "The Nonaggression
   Axiom" (p. 23 in the Mises Institute edition)
   - Original: "The libertarian creed rests upon one central axiom: that no man or group of men may aggress
     against the person or property of anyone else."
   - <https://mises.org/online-book/new-liberty-libertarian-manifesto/chapter-2-property-and-exchange/nonaggression-axiom>

6. **F. A. Hayek**, *The Road to Serfdom* (1944), ch. 6 "Planning and the Rule of Law"
   (University of Chicago Press, pp. 80–92); Czech edition *Cesta do otroctví*
   - Original: "Hence the familiar fact that the more the state "plans," the more difficult planning becomes
     for the individual." The site quotes it from "the more…" with an ellipsis.
   - Checked in: <https://aspeninstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/files/content/docs/HAYEK_ROAD_TO_SERFDOM_CH._6_(AS08).PDF>
   - Hayek was a classical liberal, not an anarchist.

7. **Eric Hughes**, *A Cypherpunk's Manifesto* (9 March 1993)
   - Original: "We cannot expect governments, corporations, or other large, faceless organizations to grant us
     privacy out of their beneficence." … "We must defend our own privacy if we expect to have any."
   - <https://www.activism.net/cypherpunk/manifesto.html>

8. **Urza**, #ne_poslušnosti, Stoky Svobodného přístavu
   - Original (Czech): "společným jmenovatelem #ne_poslušnosti je kontrast mezi respektem k lidem a nerespektem
     k platné legislativě."
   - <https://stoky.urza.cz/nep/>
   - The same page says the aim is to make viewers think, "určitě ne ke slepému následování". #ne_poslušnosti
     is Urza's video series of peaceful, public breaches of laws that punish victimless crimes; MIA is not part
     of it.

## Removed after checking

- Hayek, "'Emergencies' have always been the pretext…" (*Law, Legislation and Liberty*, vol. 3): only found in
  quote collections; the book is not freely available to check the wording.
- Urza, „Ke svobodě nemůžu nikoho donutit.“: a Finmag headline; the interview text could not be retrieved to
  confirm these are his words.
- Hughes, "Cypherpunks write code.": correct, replaced by the sentence about governments and corporations,
  which matches the manifesto section of the page.
